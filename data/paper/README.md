Individual success labels, repository clusters, seeds and confidence intervals are not available in these files. Missing numeric cells are `null`. CSV exports do not increase the precision of the printed table. For example, a difference between two one-decimal rates can differ by 0.1 points from a separately rounded published contrast.

Figure 4's visual filtering annotations do not supply a complete, mutually exclusive rejection ledger. The prose describes 600 partial-effect commands, 480 mechanically replayable candidates, 360 additionally meeting oracle/reference requirements, and 240 after deduplication. Individual rejection records are unavailable.
